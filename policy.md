# Mock Bank plc: Customer Account Policy

**Policy ref:** MB-CAP-2026-04 · **Effective:** 1 December 2026 · **Applies to:** Mock Everyday Current Account, Mock Premier Account, Mock Saver Account

> Mock Bank is a fictional bank. This policy is for illustration and testing only.

---

## 1. Fees and charges

| Item | Everyday | Premier |
|---|---|---|
| Monthly account fee | £0 | £15.00 (waived if £5,000+ paid in each month) |
| Arranged overdraft interest | 39.9% EAR | 19.9% EAR |
| Unarranged overdraft | Not permitted. Payments are declined | Not permitted |
| Returned/declined payment fee | £0 | £0 |
| CHAPS payment (branch or phone) | £25.00 | £10.00 |
| Same-day international payment (SWIFT) | £20.00 + 2.75% FX fee | £0 + 1.5% FX fee |
| Card purchase abroad (non-GBP) | 2.99% of transaction | Free |
| Cash withdrawal abroad | 2.99% + £1.50 per withdrawal | Free (up to £500/month), then 1.5% |
| Paper statement copy | £5.00 per statement | Free |
| Banker's draft | £15.00 | £10.00 |
| Bank reference/status letter | £10.00 | Free |

Faster Payments, Direct Debits, standing orders and UK ATM withdrawals are **free** on all accounts. We give **at least 2 months' notice** of any fee change.

## 2. Card replacement

| Reason | Fee | Delivery |
|---|---|---|
| Expired card (automatic renewal) | Free | Sent 3 weeks before expiry |
| Lost or stolen card | Free (first in 12 months), then £5.00 | 3–5 working days |
| Damaged card | Free | 3–5 working days |
| Express delivery (UK only) | £15.00 | Next working day if ordered before 4pm |
| Emergency card abroad | £30.00 (free for Premier) | Within 2 working days |

- Report lost or stolen cards **immediately** in the app or on **0800 000 0000** (24/7). The card is frozen straight away.
- You are not liable for transactions made after you report a loss. Before you report it, your liability is capped at **£35**, unless you acted fraudulently or with gross negligence.
- Your digital card is available in the app and in Apple Pay/Google Pay **immediately** after a replacement is ordered.
- The PIN stays the same for renewed cards. A new PIN is sent separately for lost or stolen replacements.

## 4. Transfer limits

| Payment type | Everyday | Premier |
|---|---|---|
| Faster Payment (app/online), per transaction | £10,000 | £25,000 |
| Faster Payment, daily total | £25,000 | £50,000 |
| New payee, first payment | £1,000 (held for 4 hours for checks) | £2,500 |
| Standing order, per payment | £10,000 | £25,000 |
| CHAPS (branch/phone), per transaction | No limit (ID checks apply) | No limit |
| International (SWIFT), daily total | £10,000 | £50,000 |
| ATM cash withdrawal, daily | £500 | £1,000 |
| Contactless, per transaction | £100 | £100 |
| Cash deposit at Post Office, daily | £2,000 | £5,000 |

- Daily limits reset at **midnight UK time**.
- Payments may be paused for fraud checks under the APP scam reimbursement rules. Eligible victims are reimbursed **up to £85,000** per claim.
- You can request a temporary limit increase in branch or by phone, with ID checks. It lasts up to 7 days.

## 5. Complaints

Contact us in the app, in branch or on 0800 000 0001. We aim to resolve complaints within **3 working days** and will send a final response within **8 weeks**. If you are unhappy with our response, you can refer it to the **Financial Ombudsman Service**.
